# AppstoreSpy API

> App market intelligence for Google Play and the Apple App Store: search, analytics, rankings, reviews and developer data over REST and MCP, authenticated with an API-KEY header.

## Agents

- [MCP server](https://api.appstorespy.com/mcp): Streamable HTTP MCP endpoint; send your API-KEY header
- [Agentic access](https://api.appstorespy.com/agentic-access): What each operation costs and whether it reads, queues or writes
- [Agent skills](https://api.appstorespy.com/skills/): One SKILL.md per capability
